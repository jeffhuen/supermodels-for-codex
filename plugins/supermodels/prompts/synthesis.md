# Synthesis Guide

Codex owns synthesis after the providers complete their blind first pass.

Group results by:

- Consensus findings.
- Provider-specific findings.
- Disagreements or contradictory recommendations.
- Likely false positives or weakly supported claims.
- Overcomplication and non-surgical-change findings.
- Suggested next steps.

Do not run provider-vs-provider cross-examination in version 1.
